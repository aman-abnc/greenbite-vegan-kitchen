The Story Behind GreenBite

Empowering a compassionate lifestyle through delicious, healthy, and 100% plant-based meals in Jaipur.

Kadvi Sachchai: Kya Hum Is Dard Ko Mehsoos Kar Sakte Hain?

Duniya bhar mein meat aur dairy industry ki wajah se har saal **arbon bekesoor janwaron** ko sirf swaad ke liye kata jata hai. Agar hum aakdo (statistics) ki baat karein, toh har saal duniya bhar mein 70 arab se zyada land animals (jaise murgiyan, bakriyan, suar aur gaye) aur kharabon samudri jeev sirf insaan ki thali tak pahunchne ke liye apni jaan ganwa dete hain.

Har din karodon janwaron ko dardnak aur amaanviye sthitiyon mein mara jata hai. Kya yeh zaroori hai? Bilkul nahi! Jab hamare paas plants par aadharit (plant-based) aur poshtik bhojan ke behtareen vikalp maujood hain, toh hum kisi bejubaan ki jaan lene ka karan kyun bane?

Our Mission

To make plant-based eating accessible, mouth-watering, and completely transparent. We believe that food should nourish your body without causing harm to animals or the environment.

Our Values

Every ingredient we source is organic, ethical, and local. From rich cashew cheeses to freshly harvested vegetables, sustainability is at the heart of everything we cook.
